# Gear Stats

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Shorter stat lines, and what they are worth on your class.**

A long green sentence such as "Equip: Restores 5 mana per 5 sec." becomes "+5 mp5". A sentence that names both healing and spell damage becomes two lines, Healing Power first. Haste becomes "+1% Haste". A bonus against one creature type becomes "+12 Spell Damage vs Undead" or "+9 Attack Power vs Humanoids". "Enchanted: Stamina +1 and Armor +8" becomes "+1 Stamina and +8 Armor". A named enchant stays as written. The replace-item summary says "mp5" and "Health per 5 sec". Strength and Agility say what they turn into: attack power, ranged attack power, and armor, for the class you are playing. Attack power says how much Strength that is, and how much Agility when the class gets melee attack power from it. Spirit shows `(30 / 9.9 mp5)`, at rest then while casting. Mana per 5 seconds says how much Spirit that is, the same way. The split follows the five-second rule and your meditation talent.

`Use:` and `Chance on hit:` are left as the game wrote them.

Crit from Agility is left off. The public level tables do not match Forever's own numbers, and a guessed figure would be wrong. Cat Form is not given a separate Agility conversion; a druid sees the normal class figures.

In combat the client hides regen. The note keeps the last reading from outside combat and marks it with a tilde.

Open Options, or type `/gs`. **Shorten stat lines** and **Show stat values** can each be turned off. The minimap button opens settings.
